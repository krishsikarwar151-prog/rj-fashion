Quick Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast is global shipping, and how do I track my order?
Orders placed on weekdays before 2:00 PM EST ship same-day from our fulfillment facilities. Standard shipping typically takes 2-4 business days within North America and 4-7 business days internationally. You can track your real-time status under the Track Order page.
What is your 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee policy?
We offer unconditional returns within 30 days of delivery. As long as items are in their original packaging with proof of purchase, we provide a prepaid return label and issue a 100% refund upon receipt.
Are the smartwatches and electronics covered under warranty?
Yes! All electronics and timepieces purchased through RJ Fashion come standard with a comprehensive 2-Year International Manufacturer Warranty covering manufacturing defects and electrical malfunctions.
Which promotional discount coupon codes can I use?
You can use RJ20 for 20% off your entire cart, SAVE10 for a flat $10 discount, or FREESHIP for complimentary priority delivery! Enter any of these codes during cart review or checkout.
